# 🏥 Healthcare Appointment Optimization System

> A Business Intelligence project focused on reducing appointment no-shows, improving doctor utilization, optimizing scheduling, and enhancing healthcare operations using SQL and Power BI.

---

# 📌 Project Overview

Healthcare organizations often struggle with appointment cancellations, patient no-shows, long waiting times, and inefficient doctor utilization.

This project analyzes appointment data to identify operational bottlenecks and provides actionable recommendations to improve efficiency and patient satisfaction.

The solution combines:

- SQL for business analysis
- Power BI for interactive dashboards
- Business Analysis techniques
- Functional Requirement Documentation (FRD)

# 📈 Key Business Insights

## Appointment Performance

- Total Appointments: **20,000**
- Completed: **6,724 (33.6%)**
- Cancelled: **6,583 (32.9%)**
- No Show: **6,693 (33.5%)**

📌 Nearly **66% of appointments do not result in completed consultations**, indicating significant operational inefficiencies.

---

## Peak Appointment Hour

The highest appointment volume occurs at **6:00 AM**.

### Recommendation

- Increase doctor availability
- Increase reception staffing
- Allocate additional consultation rooms

---

## Waiting Time

Average Waiting Time:

**22.75 Days**

Long waiting periods contribute to:

- Higher cancellations
- More no-shows
- Lower patient satisfaction

---

## Cancellation Analysis

Top cancellation reasons:

- Double Booking
- Emergency
- Sick Child
- Family Issue

Business observation:

Double Booking is the most controllable operational issue and can be significantly reduced through scheduling improvements.

---

## Department Analysis

Departments with the highest no-show rates:

- Orthopedics
- Pediatrics
- ENT
- General Medicine
- Neurology

Recommendation:

Implement automated reminders and appointment confirmations for high-risk departments.

---

## Doctor Utilization

Appointment distribution across doctors is uneven.

Recommendation:

Use intelligent appointment allocation to balance workload and improve resource utilization.
